I’m a cybersecurity student documenting labs, write-ups, and the fundamentals behind them.

The aim is to make the digital world less mysterious. Whether you’re starting from zero or just curious about how attacks work, I break the ideas down into everyday language — practical knowledge for staying safer online, without needing a computer science degree first.

I keep the labs on GitHub, write the guides here, and I’m lining up short videos that walk through the same concepts. This is the start of that record: what I build, what I learn, and what I’d want a hiring manager or a beginner to be able to follow.
