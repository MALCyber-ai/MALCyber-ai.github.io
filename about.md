I’m a cybersecurity student working to make the digital world less mysterious. I break complex topics into plain language, whether you’re new to the field or curious about how attacks actually work.

I document technical builds on GitHub and write guides on this site. Short videos are on the way. The aim is practical knowledge for staying safer online, written so you don’t need a computer science degree to follow it.

### What you’ll find here

- Hands-on labs and setup notes, starting with Linux
- Write-ups that show the steps and the reason they matter
- Video explainers, once the channel is live

The work in progress lives on [GitHub]({{ site.github_url }}).
